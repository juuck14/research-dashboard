# Root decision and intervention protocol

2026-10-23. This protocol distinguishes exploration from the confirmation work that follows it. It is not retrospectively presented as external preregistration.

## Before branch execution

The parent assigned three genuinely different attempted repairs: task-neutral finite-label utility; global/energy mechanisms; an independently justified universal representation target. An independent critic reads primary papers and checks the mathematical/implementation claims. A repair is not accepted just because an exact formula or new seed is added.

## Revision after the first analytical results

The old square-window utility route failed its fixed task-neutral comparisons. Two independent derivations identified the regular simplex as a better target because it optimizes symmetric task/robust-separation criteria. The next candidate is a separation between the unique optimum of a fixed uniform true-negative risk and the stability of the representation-dependent update.

The chosen low-floor exponential witness is a constructive theorem example, not a parameter chosen by searching observed accuracy. The analytic conditions must hold on an open parameter region and for arbitrary even class counts. Candidate filtering excludes every same-class negative, so a false-negative explanation cannot carry the result. Nonidentity positive views are checked separately.

## Decisive claims that must survive

1. The reference geometry has a declared task/robustness justification independent of the stability calculation.
2. The matched frozen law at that reference has it as unique global optimum, not merely another local attractor.
3. An exact full-tangent criterion covers arbitrary smooth score-policy response at that reference and all finite counts.
4. A positive-floor, true-negative-only witness has an attracting class-merging manifold in sufficient output dimension; finite residual distinctions are not called exact information erasure.
5. Random-start comparisons with the matched uniform law use the same initialization, count and execution controls. Frozen law from a random point is not substituted for the optimum's matched law.
6. Primary literature and the strongest simpler explanation must be checked. Generic performative feedback, inverse-temperature rewriting, ETF geometry or known task-dependent risk cannot separately carry the novelty.

If a core claim fails, investigate its cause and a justified alternative. Do not call an unperformed essential result optional. If all natural repair routes reduce to old knowledge or fail the declared objective, rejection must identify those attempts and its limited scope.

States are the ETF, its generic normalized perturbation, the exact balanced antipodal point and a perturbation of that point. It clarifies that uncorrected sampling changes the update/risk; the phenomenon must not be described as failure to optimize an unchanged uniform objective.
