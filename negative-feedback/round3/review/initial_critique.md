# Why the round2 progress verdict does not establish the new target

## Mathematical result versus contribution

Round2 contains a coherent exact calculation: a full tangent spectrum at one four-state square, strict K monotonicity for a response threshold, value conditions controlling other modes, and an open natural-exponential parameter region. The independent audit supports its correctness. The current task does not require disputing that mathematics to reject the leap to a top-journal contribution.

The extra knowledge is currently a quantitative sign within a designed equilibrium. Readers already know that negative sampling changes which features are useful, that adaptive weighting changes curvature, that finite negative count changes the expected objective, and that stationarity does not imply attraction. To matter at the requested level, the exact sign must expose a broader structural principle, an important overlooked limit, or a consequence that cannot be predicted adequately from those existing accounts. This is a missing contribution argument, not an allegation that an identical formula already exists.

## The semantic gap has an exact diagnostic

For the tied family f_theta(a,b)=(cos(theta)a,sin(theta)b), theta in(0,pi/2), the mapping is injective. Consequently each noiseless bit has mutual information log2 with the representation at every such theta. A growing imbalance does not by itself change that information.

Under a declared isotropic Gaussian feature channel xi~N(0,sigma²I), the Bayes factor accuracies are exactly Phi(cos(theta)/sigma) and Phi(sin(theta)/sigma). Indeed each corresponding coordinate is an equally weighted two-Gaussian sign problem, and the other factor is independent. This is a direct derivation, not a new theorem claimed as a contribution. It gives a proper consequence measure for a terminal branch.

The previous natural-policy K4 trajectory starts with an angle perturbation about1e-5 and grows it by about20. At sigma=.05, both resulting factor accuracies remain effectively perfect. Those trajectories confirm the eigenvalue, but do not yet demonstrate an important loss of task access. They cannot be counted twice as evidence of both the formula and a substantial semantic consequence.

## The old neural bridge addresses a different question

Its36 MLP forks show that free lookup parameters are not required for compression under one-negative easy filtering. The strong clean/noisy gate failed, while OLS partially recovers clean information. This is informative and honestly reported. It nevertheless does not test the joint-K law. Its presence in the same manuscript does not make the theory and experiment verify one central claim.

## What could change this assessment

A quantitatively validated mode/basin/consequence relation beyond the specially chosen square; a structural theorem whose hypotheses apply to more than one geometry and give useful predictions; or learned-encoder joint-K results showing the predicted direction and explaining deviations. Their relevance must be judged by the journal comparison fixed in advance. Generalization need not be universal or image-scale, but another numerical restatement of the square Hessian is insufficient.
