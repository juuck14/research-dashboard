# Mathematical source audit and what is left to claim

This audit separates an existing proof principle from the contrastive-specific statement. Primary full text was read; no novelty conclusion is based on title search alone.

## Directly inspected closest mathematical source

Susan Athey. *Characterizing Properties of Stochastic Objective Functions*. MIT working paper, first draft1994, revised August1999. [Primary full PDF](https://web.mit.edu/athey/www/CPSO99.pdf), Section3.3.1, especially Corollary5.3 on printed page27.

The corollary characterizes when integration preserves a zero-crossing sign. Monotonicity of the likelihood ratio is a sufficient distribution-order condition. The direct comparison proof in our Section4 is an application of this established principle. We therefore claim neither a new comparative-statics theorem nor a new general MLR preservation lemma. The paper does not analyze contrastive objectives or negative sampling.

## Strong competing explanations that remain relevant

1. At K infinity, effective-temperature or robust reweighting already explains the weighted sign of curvature. The numerical value of that limit is not a sufficient novelty claim. The finite-count no-rescue implication and no re-entry have to remain visible.
2. Finite K changes the expected population update, not only its variance. The result must not be presented as generic optimization failure at a fixed objective.
3. Graph-Laplacian curvature already explains why weak positive mixing permits within-class view directions to spread. The additional candidate result is the exact count-ordering of the required gap and its conflict with semantic-mode stabilization, not the graph decomposition itself.
4. A true result for an exchange-symmetric scalar mode does not establish a generic representation mechanism, full-space stability, information loss, or a sufficiently important paper. The companion normal-mode theorem explicitly shows that different blocks can move in opposite directions with K.
