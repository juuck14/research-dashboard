# Verified core references and their role

Inspected versions and exact relevant sections are documented in [journal/source_audit.md](journal/source_audit.md), [graph_extension_audit.md](journal/graph_extension_audit.md), [review/source_audit.md](review/source_audit.md), and the earlier [round2 prior-art audit](../round2/prior-art/literature_audit.md). A bibliographic listing is not evidence that every argument in the paper was independently proved. Some inspected versions are author arXiv manuscripts rather than byte-identical final PDFs.

## Fixed journal comparators

1. **Ji, Deng, Nakada, Zou, Zhang.** *The Power of Contrast for Feature Learning: A Theoretical Analysis*. JMLR24(330),2023. [Published original](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume24/21-1501/21-1501.pdf). A restricted theory changes understanding by separating recovery upper/lower bounds under the same assumptions; this sets a quality comparator, not a theorem contradicted here.
2. **Neumayer, Chizat, Unser.** *On the Effect of Initialization: The Scaling Path of 2-Layer Neural Networks*. JMLR25(15),2024. [Published original](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume25/23-0549/23-0549.pdf). A structural variational theory can justify small experiments without requiring practical-image performance.
3. **Huang et al.** *Understanding Whitening Loss in Self-Supervised Learning*. TPAMI46(12):9479–9492,2024. [Author-hosted published original](https://faculty.ucmerced.edu/mhyang/papers/pami2024_whitening.pdf). Separates existing explanations and links the distinction to methods; the visual-method bar remains unmet by our local theory.
4. **Zhang, Wang, Wang.** *An Augmentation Overlap Theory of Contrastive Learning*. JMLR26(228),2025. [Official publication](https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/22-1009.html), [inspected author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03114v1). **Close:** graph conditions and explicit epsilon-alignment lead to downstream guarantees; local dynamic feasibility is a different question. The oversized final PDF was not inspected directly.
5. **Li, Neuman.** *Consistency of Augmentation Graph and Network Approximability in Contrastive Learning*. JMLR27(178),2026. [Published original](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume27/25-1466/25-1466.pdf). **Close:** graph consistency and realizability; does not establish our count-indexed learning stability.

## Closest principles and competing explanations

6. **Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner, Hardt.** *Performative Prediction*. ICML2020. [Official original](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/perdomo20a.html). Distribution response and fixed-distribution optimization are different; generic response derivatives are inherited knowledge.
7. **Athey.** *Characterizing Properties of Stochastic Objective Functions*. Revised1999 MIT working paper, later published2002. [Inspected1999 original](https://web.mit.edu/athey/www/CPSO99.pdf), §3.3.1 Corollary5.3. **Closest mathematical principle:** single-crossing aggregation under likelihood-ratio shifts is not new here.
8. **Robinson et al.** *Can contrastive learning avoid shortcut solutions?* NeurIPS2021. [Inspected author original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9089441/). **Close feature-policy explanation:** suppression and feature-distinguishing global optima already depend on negative relations. Our local geometric result is not a new definition of suppression.
9. **Awasthi, Dikkala, Kamath.** *Do More Negative Samples Necessarily Hurt In Contrastive Learning?* ICML2022. [Published original](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/awasthi22b/awasthi22b.pdf). **Close count comparison:** population optima/generalization under a fixed class law; our local feedback result is not a counterexample to that theorem.
10. **HaoChen, Wei, Gaidon, Ma.** *Provable Guarantees for Self-Supervised Deep Learning with Spectral Contrastive Loss*. NeurIPS2021. [Published original](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/27debb435021eb68b3965290b5e24c49-Paper.pdf). Graph spectra and downstream guarantees are established; graph diagonalization itself cannot carry novelty.
11. **Tan et al.** *Contrastive Learning is Spectral Clustering on Similarity Graph*. ICLR2024. [Published original](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=hLZQTFGToA). **Close objective decomposition:** attraction/repulsion and graph interpretation are old; count ordering and two-boundary conjunction are the additional claims.
12. **Tschannen et al.** *On Mutual Information Maximization for Representation Learning*. ICLR2020. [Inspected original](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.13625). Non-iid negatives can invalidate usual MI conclusions. Our correlation construction only identifies a precise limitation of this paper's iid theorem.
13. **Cai, Zhang, Liu, Shi.** *The Geometric Mechanics of Contrastive Representation Learning: Alignment Potentials, Entropic Dispersion, and Cross-Modal Divergence*. ICML2026. [Official record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/cai26o.html), [inspected published PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/cai26o/cai26o.pdf). **Recent close geometry:** large-batch fixed-data-law geometry differs from finite-K detached closed-loop classification. No claim of refuting its energy results.
14. **Li, Wei, Yang, Ying.** *Statistical Consistency and Generalization of Contrastive Representation Learning*. ICML2026. [Verified official record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/li26hg.html), [inspected primary v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02116v3). **Recent count/generalization:** fixed sampling laws and statistical approximation, rather than the response Jacobian of a representation-dependent sampler. The final and v3 PDFs were not byte-compared.

The review also examined Ash et al., hard-negative sampling, diversity and loss-landscape theories; the exact inspected versions and access limitations are in the source audits. We do not claim an exhaustive impossibility proof that no prior paper anywhere implies our results.
