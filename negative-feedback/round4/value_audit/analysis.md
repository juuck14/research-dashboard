# Independent value audit: exact stability is not yet an important learning barrier

2026-10-23. This audit does not overturn the checked round3 mathematics. It withdraws the inference from that mathematics to an established JMLR-level contribution and core50 completion.

## Verdict

**Publication-level verdict: hold / additional core research required.** Keep the proved local theory and code. The current evidence does not justify a months-long top-journal paper investment without a bounded, decisive learning-meaning or broader structural result. Conversely it does not justify declaring the mathematical direction wholly worthless or impossible.

- **Proceed:** preservation, error checking, and a specifically limited next test that can alter the importance judgment.
- **Hold:** JMLR core50, generic adaptive-sampling conclusions, and the claim that remaining work is chiefly editorial.
- **Stop:** the current semantic-erasure interpretation and treating a finite stability window as a useful hyperparameter rule without an applicable learned geometry and declared learning objective.

This is not a demand for natural-image benchmarks. A theory-only contribution could meet the bar if it establishes a consequential barrier or resolves a recognized inference gap. The missing step is why this conditional local classification is such a result, not whether it is mathematically sharp.

## What remains mathematically meaningful

The all-finite-count classification is stronger than computing an infinite-count limit. The opposite normal-block ordering and complete square spectrum are genuine results. In particular this audit does not claim the formulas are direct corollaries of every prior paper merely because they use known mathematical tools. Novelty remains plausible within the declared family.

But all-finite quantification can sharpen an unimportant object as well as an important one. A very exact characterization of the feasibility of a chosen equilibrium is not a characterization of a desirable representation unless its desirability is independently specified. The original semantic-erasure justification failed, so the replacement justification has to do new work.

## An actual task-meaning countercheck

See `protocol.md` recorded before `task_meaning_check.py`, and the actual output `task_meaning_check.json`. The first launch found a syntax-only missing-space error and was corrected without changing settings or criteria. The executed checker uses the inherited population gradient oracle, so it is not an independent gradient implementation.

Let X=(a,b,v) be three independent signs and use two observed views per semantic class(a,b). The declared family is

\[
f_\alpha(X)=\left(\sqrt{(1-\alpha^2)/2}\,a,\sqrt{(1-\alpha^2)/2}\,b,\alpha v\right).
\]

At alpha0 the positive gap.05 and natural epsilon.3 policy give a view-normal direction z=(0,0,v). Direct field differences at steps1e-5 and1e-4 show its restoring value is about **+.00267594 atK8** and **−.00257998 atK16**, for both frozen and adaptive samplers. It really is one of the directions involved in the claimed upper boundary, rather than an unrelated geometric perturbation.

For every0<alpha<1, signs of the three coordinates recover a,b,v linearly and exactly. Hence

\[
I(a;f_\alpha)=I(b;f_\alpha)=1\text{ bit},\qquad
I(v;f_0)=0,\ I(v;f_\alpha)=1,
\]

and I(X;f_alpha) rises from2to3bits. Thus the local unstable direction can add accessible information while preserving the two original semantic bits. This does not establish the nonlinear flow endpoint, but it disproves inferring information harm from the direction's instability alone.

Under the **explicit extra evaluation assumption** of additive isotropic Gaussian noise sigma, exact Bayes accuracies are

\[
A_{a,b}=\Phi\!\left(\sqrt{(1-\alpha^2)/2}/\sigma\right),\qquad A_v=\Phi(\alpha/\sigma).
\]

At sigma.2, alpha.3 slightly reduces the semantic accuracy while greatly improving the view-bit accuracy. A weighted downstream objective can therefore prefer or disprefer the same perturbation. The view task is not positive-invariant; that fact is explicit and is why the next check is separate.

### A task-consistent version of the same objection

Use the existing K4 attracting rectangle theta=.7545192717973. Tasks a andb are both constant within each positive class, so both are compatible with the positive channel. At sigma.5, the balanced representation gives.9213504accuracy for either task. The rectangle gives.9274697for a and.9146373for b.

If the declared task weight of a is.9, utility improves from.9213504to.9261864. If it is.1, utility decreases to.9159206. With noiseless linear access, both tasks stay perfect. Thus neither positive consistency nor exact injectivity alone picks a universal quality order. Balanced equal energy is a decision tied to task weights, noise, worst-case margin or another declared criterion.

These are familiar consequences of task-dependent representation quality. They are **not new publication contributions** and should not be used to replace one weak novelty claim with another. Their value here is falsificatory: the specific stability calculations do not supply their own learning-importance interpretation.

## Closest checked primary perspective

Tian et al., *What Makes for Good Views for Contrastive Learning?*, NeurIPS2020, [published primary PDF](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/4c2e5eaae9152079b9e95845750bb9ab-Paper.pdf). I read §3.1–3.3, especially Definition3 and Proposition3.1 plus the surrounding assumptions. Their “optimal representation” is explicitly indexed by a downstream task; the InfoMin proposition presupposes minimal sufficient encoders and preservation of the task label information. This is not a theorem about our dynamical system, but it directly prevents treating task-independent contraction or nuisance removal as an automatically established utility notion. No broader impossibility result is attributed to that paper.

## The strongest unresolved issue

The issue is not “run ImageNet.” It is whether there is an independently justified target property whose learning feasibility is newly decided by the theory. Possibilities include a declared robust semantic margin, a downstream sample-complexity cost of nuisance acquisition, or a genuinely general geometric barrier with a recognizable comparison theorem. If one of these is used, its relation to the local count law is core research, not optional illustrative benchmarking.

One precise next decision would compare an applicable learned family under fixed finite-label or noise conditions, with the objective stated before training. The test must distinguish the new response/count diagnosis from the simpler static negative-repulsion explanation; it must also admit that an unstable reference may lead to an equally good or better geometry. The present normal-direction and rectangle checks show why merely plotting growth again cannot resolve that uncertainty.
